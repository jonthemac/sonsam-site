WHAT SONSAM IS

Everything you need to have a good first year. In one chat.

Sonsam lives on WhatsApp, in the app you already open a hundred times a day. Level 100 is hard for almost everyone. Nobody should have to figure it out alone.

Somewhere to vent

The stuff you can't put in the group chat or say to your mum. No judgement, no lecture, and nobody in your hall will hear about it.

Answers at 3 am

Lost on campus, lost in a course, lost in your feelings. Ask anything, at any hour, when nobody else is awake.

Skills for your head

Two-minute exercises for the overthinking, the nerves and the homesickness. Learn to manage your thoughts, feelings and emotions, between lectures.

THE SCIENCE BIT

Behind the stickers, we take this seriously.

Sonsam has adapted cutting-edge mental-health interventions from The Lab for Scalable Mental Health at Northwestern University in the USA, and reshaped them around the language, pressures and rhythms of campus life in Ghana.

Students get them through the Sonsam chat on WhatsApp, so real, tested tools live inside the app they already use, with no new download and no waiting list.

Evidence-based

Interventions developed and tested by researchers at Northwestern's Lab for Scalable Mental Health, adapted for our context.

Where students already are

It runs on WhatsApp, works on any phone, and is there at 3 am. No app to download, no account to make.

Not a replacement for care

When someone needs more than we can give, we point them to their campus counselling centre and emergency support.
